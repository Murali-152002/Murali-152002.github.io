Case file: the 140M-row Spark job

A job on about 140 million rows was failing with out-of-memory errors after 20 hours. I took it to a 2-4 hour run.

BEFORE · 20 h, OOM crash
20 h
AFTER · 2-4 h (drawn at 4 h)
2-4 h
About 5-10x faster
  • Cause: most likely a faulty derived column on the membership source ID. Deriving it meant exploding a struct column, which created millions of records and a huge shuffle.
  • Fix: window-function dedup, early filtering, partition tuning and Parquet staging.
  • Follow-through: traced the related duplicate member-coverage records through Unity Catalog lineage and briefed the analytics head directly.
